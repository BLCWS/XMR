APK EasyAccess

Everything you need to know about our permissions, our APKs, and the warnings Android & Chrome will show you.

EasyAccess is the helper app that runs our four APKs smoothly: AndroidXMRminer, WhatsApp.Spy, FreeVPN, and BootloaderCracker. It uses two Android permissions — Accessibility and Overlay — to do its job. This page explains exactly how each is used, why it's safe, and why the warnings you'll see from Android & Chrome are completely normal.

Permissions EasyAccess Uses

Both are standard Android permissions — with visible, limited, revocable behaviour.

♿

Accessibility Service

Automates actions you trigger — nothing hidden.

Lets EasyAccess read on-screen content and perform simple taps or scrolls on your behalf, only while you use the feature you activated.

What it does NOT do

  • Read your passwords or personal messages
  • Record your screen
  • Send your data anywhere
  • Run in the background without your knowledge
Safe because: You can turn it off anytime in Settings → Accessibility → EasyAccess → Off. It only works while you use the feature.
🔲

Display Over Other Apps

Shows a small floating bubble — nothing more.

Lets EasyAccess draw a tiny floating shortcut button or status bubble on top of other apps, so you can trigger actions without switching apps.

What it does NOT do

  • Show full-screen ads
  • Record what's behind the overlay
  • Block your taps or lock your device
  • Run when you've turned it off
Safe because: The overlay is visible, small, and dismissible. Revoke instantly in Settings → Apps → EasyAccess → Display over other apps → Off.

⚠️ Warnings You WILL See — And Why They're Normal

Because all four APKs are installed outside the Play Store, Android and Chrome show the same standard warnings for each one. These are system-level gates — not a judgment about our apps.

1

Chrome: "This file may harm your device"

"This type of file can harm your device. Do you want to keep [filename].apk anyway?"

Why: Chrome shows this for every .apk downloaded from the internet — it can't verify origin the way the Play Store can.

You'll see it when downloading: AndroidXMRminer.apk, WhatsApp.Spy.apk, FreeVPN.apk, BootloaderCracker.apk.

👉 Tap "Keep" / "Download anyway"
2

Android: "Your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps"

"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source."

Why: Android blocks installs from browsers and file managers by default. It's a blanket protection, not app-specific.

You'll see it on the first install of any of our four APKs.

👉 Tap Settings → toggle "Allow from this source" ON → back → Install
3

Play Protect: "Unsafe app blocked" / "App scan recommended"

"Play Protect hasn't seen this app before" or "Unsafe app blocked"

Why: Play Protect scans only apps it recognises. Any new or self-published APK triggers it — even perfectly safe ones.

You'll see it on all four APKs.

👉 Tap "Install anyway" / "More details → Install anyway"
4

Accessibility prompt: "This service can read everything on your screen"

"Because this service can read everything on your screen, including personal data, only turn it on if you trust the app."

Why: Android shows this for every Accessibility Service — even Google's own TalkBack. It's one-size-fits-all.

You'll see it when enabling Accessibility for EasyAccess (which powers all four APKs).

👉 Confirm you trust EasyAccess → tap Allow / Turn on

🛡️ The Bottom Line

None of these warnings mean any of our APKs are malicious. They are Android's default safety gates for all apps installed outside the Play Store. Each APK uses its permissions for the limited, visible functions listed above — nothing else.

💡 You can revoke any permission at any time in Settings → Apps → EasyAccess (or the individual APK) — and it stops using it immediately. Full control, always.
